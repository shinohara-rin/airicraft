## Non-fatal bugs / workarounds

- **Tick-debug snapshot world-time mismatch.** Evidence: pause snapshot at `clientTickId=914` reported `worldTime=318`; the one-step snapshot at `clientTickId=915` reported `worldTime=640`. Workaround: use `clientTickId`, `pauseEpoch`, and snapshot IDs as the timing authority, not `worldTime`, until clarified. Impact: no current gameplay block, but it weakens time-sensitive debugging evidence.
- **Navigation can fail at the destination perimeter.** Evidence: `navigate_to(284,67,-141)` started at `(65,67,-86)`, moved the player to `(281,65,-144)`, then ended `CALCULATION_FAILED` / `Path calculation failed` with an estimated 25 ticks remaining. The target was visibly in a small azalea/oak forest. Workaround: once within local-resource range, switch to the typed resource collector rather than retrying the same exact navigation goal. Impact: non-fatal for local collection, but unreliable for objectives requiring a precise target block.
- **Tick-debug pause does not freeze hostile damage.** Evidence: after `pause` returned `paused: true`, `clientTickId=3963`, and `pauseEpoch=1`, the event stream recorded an arrow hit, fall damage, four zombie hits, death, and respawn, all at tick `3963`. The player lost the four collected logs. Workaround: do not use tick pause as a combat or real-time safety mechanism; it can still preserve a client-side snapshot for diagnosis. Impact: severe for interactive safety, but the fresh respawn leaves the survival attempt recoverable.
- **Blocked collection retained an active mine primitive (fixed live).** Evidence: wood collection declared `WAITING_FOR_PICKUP` / `target_missing` after collecting 4 of 16 logs, but its active mining task continued, moving from Y=67 to Y=44 before a hostile reflex. Cause: a blocked collection job still exposed its mine goal and retained its desired primitive request. Fix: only `RUNNING` collection jobs may expose or retain a primitive. Focused `EmbodiedAgentRuntimeTest` passed; hot-reload validation submitted a no-target one-log goal and confirmed `taskExecution=IDLE`, no goal ownership, and no movement.
- **Task lifecycle responses amplified full world evidence (fixed live).** Evidence: plain `agent tasks resume` and `agent tasks cancel` each emitted about 306k tokens, including the full known craft/smelt catalog, for a one-line lifecycle acknowledgement. Fix: the normal wrapper view now projects only lifecycle, compact reflex, compact task execution, and compact task fields; `missionExecution` remains verbose-only. `AiricraftCliMainTest` covers the omission, `:wrapper:test` passed, and the rebuilt CLI returned the bounded cancellation response against the live client. Commit: `b18376a`.
- **Survival reflex status/action race and drowning wedge.** Evidence: `agent status` reported `reflex.state=IDLE`, then an immediate `craft_recipe` was rejected as `reflex_active`. Later it stayed `AWAITING_PLANNER` for `DROWNING` with full air, no `holdId`, and no action allowed. Workaround: `airicraft reload` resets agent state while preserving the world; it restored `taskExecution=IDLE` and allowed a craft. Impact: an unattended driver cannot rely on an idle status read as an action permit, and a water reflex can halt all gameplay until reload.
- **Placement-site query treats water as `air_or_replaceable`.** Evidence: `inspect_world(find_placement_sites, targetMaterial=air_or_replaceable, requireSolidTopSupport=true, requireAirAbove=true)` returned water targets above sand, while automatic workbench setup rejected the same terrain with `crafting_table_place_failed ... no_static_feasible_site`. Workaround: request `targetMaterial=air`; however the current displaced position had no matching site. Impact: the query's advertised filter does not predict whether a non-waterloggable block can actually be placed.
- **Terminal navigation could retain a stale mutation gate (fixed live).** Evidence: after a `navigate_to` terminal `CALC_FAILED` and explicit cancellation, `agent status` showed no runnable job but `craft_recipe` was denied `active_task_in_progress` because its old `PAUSED_BY_REFLEX` execution snapshot was consulted before the terminal task/job states. Fix: terminal semantic task + terminal/idle active job now wins over stale execution state. Focused `EmbodiedAgentRuntimeTest` passed. After a client restart, the live pickaxe call reached its true `crafting_table_place_failed ... site_not_found` result rather than the stale-task denial. Commit: `7e4a1c5`.
- **`return_to_surface` can livelock when there is no remembered target and no support.** Evidence: after supplying available oak planks as tower filler, the executor stayed `RUNNING` for more than 1,400 ticks at `towering:support_unavailable`; it has no terminal path for that repeated condition. The initial default also ignored the available planks and failed `missing_filler_block` because it only tried dirt/cobblestone. Workaround: none in a deep open-water column; a visible-village navigation route was needed instead.
- **Nearest surface search is too local after reflex displacement.** The search radius was widened from 12 to 16 blocks (`f53b49e`) after it missed proven village terrain about 15 blocks away; focused `SurfaceMemoryTest` passed. The fix helps at that boundary, but a later reflex moved the player to `(-104,60,-44)`, where no forest was found in the loaded 256-block feature search and the camera showed only open ocean floor. A local surface-memory scan still cannot recover from this class of displacement.
- **Mob flee used blind movement and could enter water (fixed).** The reflex now selects loaded, dry, hazard-free standing targets that increase separation, prefers cover, reaches targets through short exact Baritone hops, and raises the configured water traversal cost while it owns the route. If water is already moving or submerging the player, it hands recovery to the existing water-aware safe-standing navigator before resuming ground flee. The deterministic water regression remains useful for exact terrain control, but live acceptance now runs in the untouched, naturally generated snowy-forest `pickup` world.
- **A short flee could falsely resolve and wait for the pursuer (fixed).** Threats are retained while alive and loaded rather than dropped at 12 blocks. Security uses each mob's own route toward the player, not L2 distance or player-origin reachability: a zombie below a three-block pillar and a skeleton behind a sealed wall can be inaccessible even when close, while ranged line of sight remains dangerous. Complete routes of at least 16 nodes require 60 stable ticks; null/partial routes require 200 ticks, so a transient partial path is not accepted as shelter. The first natural run exposed the old short confirmation by resolving at tick `1087` before the same zombie returned at `1192`; that criterion was removed.
- **Close single-mob combat now terminates the encounter (fixed live).** In the final natural-world run, a low-health reflex started `FLEE` at tick `478` and immediately emitted `reflex.flee_knockback` against the zombie at `2.05` blocks, before path selection. It switched to `DEFEND` when safe, continued cooldown-timed `reflex.close_quarter_attack` hits, earned `Monster Hunter` at tick `564`, and resolved `threats_clear` at tick `565` without a second safety epoch. The exact tick-`1577` post-combat snapshot was survival mode at `(16.57,80.0,5.38)`, alive with full health, on dry ground, and not touching or submerged in water.

## Harness gaps / discoveries (non-mission-critical)

- **Exact block break exists but was easy to miss in discovery.** `break_blocks` is an exposed, coordinate-grounded tool; it requires a fresh `inspect_world`/feature read and direct interaction range. At `(8,70,-104)`, inspection found five oak logs at `(8,69..65,-104)` and `break_blocks` completed the top four. `inspect_inventory` then confirmed `minecraft:oak_log=5`. This is the safe workaround for the earlier `mine_blocks` divergence, not a missing API.
- **Automatic workbench setup needs a feasible-site explanation.** `craft_recipe` correctly reported `crafting_table_place_failed`, but the driver needs the exact rejected terrain constraints and a preferred reachable target, not just `site_not_found`, to recover without trial-and-error.
- **Surface recovery now has a bounded water-aware route, not global shore discovery.** Mob flee and drowning recovery can reach a loaded safe-standing target without blind swimming, but `return_to_surface` still depends on remembered or locally discoverable land. A player already displaced into an open ocean beyond that bounded search still needs persistent safe-ground memory or broader shore discovery.

## Genuine blockers

- **Deep-cave mining violates the survival-readiness gate and can be fatal (observed live 2026-09-01; immediate lighting boundary fixed live).** On the fresh forest-world run, the driver first obtained wood, stone tools, a furnace, three iron ingots, and an iron pickaxe. The high-level `DIAMOND` action graph then chose `minecraft:diamond_block` instead of ore and cancelled without breaking a block. The explicit `mine_blocks` workaround correctly found three diamonds, but Baritone descended from the established Y=48 mine into an unlit cave at Y=-6 without food, armor, torches, a bed, or a safe-return plan. A skeleton-driven reflex then preempted a one-log resupply task. Tick-debug pause captured the player at Y=10 with 10.5 health, nearby skeleton/spider/zombie, and no protection; releasing the reflex for six seconds resulted in death and respawn, with the dropped progression inventory later despawned. A second `return_to_surface` from the empty recovery run again descended to Y=5, where a pause captured 8/20 health and no items. The illumination preflight had been advisory; `d456010` now rejects predicted underground mining with zero torches unless `allowUnilluminated=true`. After a full driver-client restart, a live `mine_blocks(deepslate_diamond_ore)` request returned `insufficient_illumination … torchCount=0` before Baritone received a route. This closes the immediate unlit-mining hole, but a full survival-readiness gate still needs food, armor/weapon, and a bounded exit/bed policy.
- **High-level `DIAMOND` resource mapping is wrong.** Evidence: the action graph route selected `minecraft:diamond_block:minecraft:iron_pickaxe`, then attempted a `diamond_block_to_diamond` crafting recipe and cancelled with `brokenBlocks=0`. Workaround: explicit `mine_blocks` over `minecraft:diamond_ore,minecraft:deepslate_diamond_ore` worked and collected three diamonds. Impact: normal diamond progression cannot be trusted through the intended typed goal.
- **Exact break/drop collection and path terminal reporting are weak but recoverable.** `break_blocks` can return `pending_timeout` for unarmed logs even after lower blocks are broken; drops are not proactively chased beyond pickup radius. `navigate_to` can report `CALC_FAILED` after the player is actually at its coordinates. Workaround: navigate to each known drop/target and treat observed player position as authoritative. The requested “break leaves for autocollect” is not evidenced as the cause: the live pathfinding profile already had `allowBreak=true`, and only crafting table/furnace/chest variants were protected from breaking.
- **Entity discovery is too local for bed-first survival.** `inspect_nearby_entities` is a 32-block scan. Several controlled forest sweeps found no sheep, but there is no typed entity-search/exploration primitive to pursue wool before night. A bed-first policy needs an entity acquisition route that can search safely and return to shelter, rather than falling through to cave mining.

- **Worktable recovery remains mission-blocking in the current saved state.** The player can now be driven from the original water column to the village coast: an inspected sandstone structure at `(8,54,-6)` led to a route ending on dry sand/grass around `(5,61,-3)`, even though Baritone labeled the final route `CALC_FAILED`. However, the drowning reflex subsequently displaced the player back into water. The restarted runtime has no active-task gate bug, but automatic crafting-table setup still finds no site from the water position, direct placement finds no adjacent support, and the built-in surface return can livelock. Consequently the first wooden pickaxe cannot yet be made reliably, so the run cannot advance to stone/iron/End progression without a water-aware recovery/place-site fix.
- **The existing displaced save is not automatically repaired by the new flee policy.** The change prevents a new hostile flee from blindly entering an ocean and can recover from water when loaded safe standing exists. It does not create a distant shoreline target for the already stranded save. Resume the Ender Dragon playthrough from a known dry checkpoint or add broader shore discovery before treating that exact saved state as unblocked.
